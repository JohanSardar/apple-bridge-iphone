What the app does: Apple Bridge is the iPhone companion for two open-source Mac tools. BOGO (https://github.com/JohanSardar/bogo) is an approver: when an AI agent on the owner's Mac wants to spend money or take a risky action, the Mac sends the request to this app, the owner reviews exactly what will run and approves with Face ID or denies. Apple Bridge (https://github.com/JohanSardar/apple-bridge) lets the owner's Mac ask the phone to do things they have switched on one by one, such as show a notification or read a Health summary.

No account and no sign-in: the app pairs with the owner's own Mac, which runs the BOGO daemon or the Apple Bridge gateway. A reviewer will not have one, so no request will arrive.

What you can see without a Mac:
- BOGO tab: "Connect your Mac" with an Add Mac button. Add Mac shows the command to run on the Mac and waits for it; without a Mac it keeps waiting.
- Activity tab: empty until approvals happen.
- Settings tab: BOGO's Macs, Apple Bridge pairing (a QR scanner and a field to paste a pairing code), the list of capabilities with a switch each, and Diagnostics. Each permission (Health, Location, Notifications, Screen Time and so on) is asked for only when you switch on the capability that needs it.

Nothing is collected or sent anywhere except to the owner's own paired Mac, encrypted, through a relay that sees only ciphertext.
